3. What Students Learn

Students gain industry knowledge through structured training, practical assignments and real-time implementation. They improve technical skills, communication, teamwork and problem solving ability. The internship helps students understand real workplace environments, project development process and professional practices required for career growth.

4. Project Based Training

1.Team Project

Students work together as a team to develop projects. They learn collaboration, task management, communication and real-time project workflow. Timeline: 2 - 4 Weeks

2.Individual Project

Students build their own project based on their selected domain. This improves creativity, technical confidence and problem-solving skills. Timeline: 1 - 3 Weeks

3.Live Project Training

Students work on practical industry-based projects and understand real development processes, testing and deployment. Timeline: Based on Project
